## 1. Anthropic

### Claude Sonnet 5.5 (28 September 2026)
Sources: https://www.anthropic.com/claude-sonnet-5-5 and https://www.anthropic.com/claude-sonnet-5-5-system-card
What Anthropic says:
- A clear upgrade over Claude Sonnet 5, running more than 30 percent faster and costing up to 30 percent less for most work. Strongest at well-scoped everyday tasks, fixing bugs, and creating polished documents, slides, and spreadsheets.
- Scores 70.6 percent on Terminal-Bench 4.0, an agentic coding evaluation, against Sonnet 5's 10.3 percent.
- Thinks in fewer, more robust steps. Coding evaluations show roughly a third fewer tool calls and about half the shell runs to finish a task compared with Sonnet 5, so builders wait less to see progress.
- Pricing unchanged from Sonnet 5: $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens, with up to 90 percent savings via prompt caching and 50 percent via batch processing. 1 million token context window.
- Per Anthropic's Responsible Scaling Policy evaluations, Sonnet 5.5 is broadly less capable than Opus 5.5 across domains and crosses no new RSP thresholds. Rate limits were raised across Claude Code and the Claude Platform to accommodate the model's usage.

### How to use this at Touch Stone Publishers
1. Move well-scoped, everyday production work to Sonnet 5.5 now, bug fixes on the Build-a-Class site, polishing White Paper drafts into final documents, and formatting slides and spreadsheets. It is priced the same as Sonnet 5 but faster and better suited to exactly this kind of task, freeing Opus 5.5 for the harder flagship-pipeline stages.
2. Re-run any Claude Code task that previously needed many tool calls or shell runs. Sonnet 5.5's roughly one-third fewer tool calls and half the shell runs should shorten the Build-a-Class site's iteration loop at the same token price.
3. Add Sonnet 5.5 as a fourth entrant in the coding bake-off from earlier issues, alongside Opus 5.5, GPT-6 Sol and Luna, and Grok 4.7, specifically for the well-scoped tasks it is positioned for rather than the hardest ones.

## 3. Meta

### Launching Meta Enterprise Platform (29 September 2026)
Source: https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/launching-meta-enterprise-platform/
What Meta says:
- A new business unit bringing Meta's full AI technology stack, the Muse agent, Meta Business Agent, Muse API, and Muse Code, to businesses and developers to help them grow. Built on Meta's advanced models, agents, large-scale infrastructure, and years of working with businesses.
- Chirantan "CJ" Desai joins Meta as Chief Enterprise Platform Officer, reporting directly to Mark Zuckerberg. He was previously CEO and President of MongoDB, led product and engineering at Cloudflare, and spent nearly eight years at ServiceNow including as President and COO.
- Meta states security and privacy are built into its enterprise products from the outset, and that the platform will turn Meta's AI stack into products and services companies can deploy for their own businesses.

### Muse for Small Business (Connect week; not in earlier issues)
Source: https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/introducing-muse-small-business/
What Meta says:
- Muse can connect a business's Instagram professional account analytics, Facebook Pages, and Meta ad accounts in a few clicks, plus dozens of tools businesses already run on, including Asana, Box, Canva, Dropbox, Figma, HighLevel, Intuit QuickBooks, Klaviyo, Notion, Shopify, Slack, Stripe, and Zoom.
- Meta's pages describe multiple subscription tiers for Muse and Meta One access, but a single, clear price list for the small-business tier specifically was not resolved in search. Treat any dollar figure for this tier as unconfirmed until checked directly on Meta's page.

### How to use this at Touch Stone Publishers
1. Read the Meta Enterprise Platform launch as a leadership signal, not a today decision. Hiring a CEO-level enterprise executive to report to Zuckerberg means Meta intends to compete for the same business-AI budget as OpenAI's Work tier and Anthropic's Claude Partner Network. Revisit in a quarter once the platform has pricing and case studies.
2. Test Muse for Small Business's tool connections against the marketing engine's current stack. Shopify, Canva, and Slack are already in TSP's workflow; a native Muse connection to those tools is worth a hands-on trial once the small-business tier's pricing is confirmed.
3. Note the Muse Business Agent and Muse Code as a second data point, after Astra for Law and Anthropic's Life Sciences Verification Program, on vendors racing to package frontier AI for specific business functions rather than general chat.
