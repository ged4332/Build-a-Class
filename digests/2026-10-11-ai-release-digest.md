## 2. OpenAI

### Sharing AI progress in mathematics (6 October 2026; not in earlier issues)
Source: https://openai.com/index/sharing-ai-progress-in-mathematics/
What OpenAI says:
- OpenAI published new results on open problems in mathematics generated by an internal frontier model, sharing Lean proof formalizations and research details in a GitHub repository rather than a product announcement.
- OpenAI consulted the Institute for Advanced Study's Advisory Group on Mathematics and Artificial Intelligence on how to share the results responsibly. The repository includes summaries of the model's reasoning and compute estimates; the average result needed roughly three hours of ChatGPT Pro-equivalent thinking.
- More formal Lean verifications are planned to be added over time.

### Helping teens learn, plan, and shape the future of AI (7 October 2026; not in earlier issues)
Source: https://openai.com/index/teens-learn-and-plan/
What OpenAI says:
- College Planner is coming to ChatGPT for Teens: it brings together application requirements, deadlines, tasks, and financial-aid steps for a student's school list in one plan. The initial US version targets students in grades 10 through 12.
- New flashcards and quizzes let students upload class notes or pick a topic, and ChatGPT turns the material into review cards.
- OpenAI is supporting Boston Children's Hospital's Digital Wellness Lab and its Student Advisory Council for three years; for the 2026-27 school year the Lab convenes roughly 22 students across two tracks to help shape OpenAI's approach for teens.

### How to use this at Touch Stone Publishers
1. Log the math-research publication as a sector-brief data point for Technology on frontier labs treating open mathematical problems as a visible capability benchmark, worth a line next to any brief on AI research credibility.
2. Note College Planner and the teen flashcard tools as a Content Creation Lab talking point on where consumer AI education features are heading, useful if the lab ever markets to parents or education-adjacent small businesses.
