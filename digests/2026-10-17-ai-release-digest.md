## 2. OpenAI

### Bringing GPT-Rosalind to academic research labs (15 October 2026; not in earlier issues)
Source: https://openai.com/index/introducing-the-rosalind-academic-plan/
What OpenAI says:
- The Rosalind Academic Plan gives eligible labs at US universities up to 30 Business Standard seats at no cost for up to 12 months, including sponsored access to GPT-Rosalind, OpenAI's life sciences model, subject to trusted-access approval.
- The plan also includes access to all commercially available frontier OpenAI models within plan usage limits, but excludes API access and API credits. Research data shared through the plan is not used to train OpenAI's models by default, though OpenAI's help center notes Zero Data Retention is not available under this offer.
- Principal investigators or lab leads at universities without an existing OpenAI Enterprise agreement can apply directly; labs at universities that already have an agreement apply through their university's Enterprise access process. OpenAI says it plans to expand the program to other countries in the future.
