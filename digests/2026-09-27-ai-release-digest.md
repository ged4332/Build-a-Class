## 1. Anthropic

### Life Sciences Verification Program (17 September 2026; not in earlier issues)
Source: https://www.anthropic.com/news/life-sciences-verification-program
What Anthropic says:
- The Life Sciences Verification Program gives verified life science professionals access to Mythos, Opus, and Sonnet models with a refined, more permissive set of safeguards for biology-related work.
- Launching in beta for teams and institutions first, with plans to expand to individual Pro and Max plans over time. Dozens of organizations were already onboarded through early access, with hundreds more expected to enroll in the first week of the broader opening.
- Eligible use cases include drug discovery, research biology, clinical development, and manufacturing, open to academic labs, startups, and pharma companies. Each applicant goes through verification covering research credentials, security standards, and ethical research oversight.

### Partnering with Accenture on embedded evaluation (18 September 2026; not in earlier issues)
Source: https://www.anthropic.com/news/accenture-embedded-evaluation
What Anthropic says:
- A partnership with Accenture on independent evaluation of frontier AI, embedding evaluators at Anthropic. Both organizations expect to invest at least $1 billion over the next five years to build capacity in this area.

### Snowflake and Anthropic expand partnership to $200 million (22 September 2026; not in earlier issues)
Source: https://www.anthropic.com/news/snowflake-anthropic-expanded-partnership
What Anthropic says:
- A multi-year, $200 million agreement makes Claude models available in the Snowflake platform to more than 12,600 global customers across Amazon Bedrock, Google Cloud Vertex AI, and Microsoft Azure.
- A joint go-to-market initiative targets AI agents that handle complex, multi-step analysis, powered by Claude's reasoning and Snowflake's governed data environment. Claude Sonnet 4.5 already powers Snowflake Intelligence, an enterprise agent that answers from structured and unstructured data in natural language.

### Claude discovers a novel enzyme system (23 September 2026; not in earlier issues)
Source: https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-discovers-novel-enzyme-system
What Anthropic says:
- In early results from a new life sciences research lab formed this spring, Claude agents found a novel enzyme system with properties reminiscent of CRISPR, whose function is still unknown, working from only high-level direction from Anthropic's scientists.
