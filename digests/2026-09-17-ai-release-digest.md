## 2. OpenAI

### ChatGPT Ads: Sponsored Agents, prompt-built ads, HubSpot and Shopify (16 September 2026)
Sources: https://openai.com/index/reimagining-advertising-with-ai/ and https://ads.openai.com/
What OpenAI says:
- Sponsored Agents, now in testing: after seeing a relevant ad in ChatGPT, a user can choose to start a clearly labeled conversation with a business-sponsored agent, explain what matters to them, ask follow-up questions, and follow a link to the business's website when ready to take the next step.
- Ad creation: businesses can create ads by writing a few prompts in ChatGPT Work. Ads Manager gains new AI creative tools.
- HubSpot is the first CRM partner. Businesses managing customers in HubSpot can connect a ChatGPT Ads account, create ads, track performance, and follow up on leads inside HubSpot.
- Shopify is the first ecommerce partner. US-based Shopify merchants can use a ChatGPT Ads app in the Shopify App Store to create and manage ChatGPT ad campaigns.
- OpenAI states ChatGPT Ads reached $1 billion in annualized revenue run rate in under 200 days from launch.
- Pricing and targeting details were not in the search results. OpenAI's ad policies page governs what can run: https://openai.com/policies/ad-policies/

### How to use this at Touch Stone Publishers
1. Treat Sponsored Agents as the Content Creation Lab's next intake channel. The lab already qualifies buyers through an application form. A sponsored agent that answers seat, date, and price questions and then hands off to the form is the same flow inside ChatGPT. Ask to join the test before it opens broadly.
2. Draft the lab ad in ChatGPT Work and compare it against the marketing-engine output. The prompt-built ad is a second draft for the CMO approval queue, not a replacement for it. Keep the platform-separation rule: this is a ChatGPT channel, not LinkedIn.
3. Decide whether HubSpot is worth adopting for outreach tracking. The outreach-review loop tracks reply and meeting rates by hand. If ChatGPT Ads leads land in HubSpot with performance data attached, the CRM becomes the single record for both cold outreach and paid leads.
4. Note the Shopify path for the Lulu print titles. US merchants can run ChatGPT ad campaigns from the Shopify App Store. If the playbook hardcovers ever sell through a Shopify storefront, this is a direct ad channel to ChatGPT's audience.
5. Use the $1 billion run-rate figure as a board-brief signal. A new ad platform reaching that scale in under 200 days is a Priority-level story for the Technology and Consumer sector rotations, and a data point for any brief on where advertising budgets are moving.
