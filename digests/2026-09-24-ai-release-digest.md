## 3. Meta

Day two of Meta Connect 2026, at Meta's Menlo Park campus.

### Muse coming to AI glasses, hands-free (Meta Connect 2026)
Source: search-derived from Meta Connect day-two coverage on meta.com and developer.meta.com; exact URL for the standalone announcement page did not resolve in search today.
What Meta says:
- Muse, Meta's personal AI agent, is coming to AI glasses "in the coming months" for fully hands-free access, activated by saying the agent's name as a wake word, with no phone needed to start a conversation.
- Stated use cases: guiding a workout in real time, logging meals and giving nutrition insights, booking an appointment, checking flight prices, and acting on what the wearer visually sees through the glasses, for example a product on a store shelf, a flyer, or a school-supplies list, without pulling out a phone.

### Ray-Ban Meta Audio (24 September 2026)
Source: search-derived from Meta's Connect day-two product announcement; the dedicated Ray-Ban Meta Audio product page did not resolve in search today.
What Meta says:
- Meta's first audio-only glasses, built with Ray-Ban. 43 grams, offered in 23 color and lens combinations across two styles, Clubmaster and Burbank.
- Battery life: up to 12 hours of AI-assistant use on a charge, plus 48 more hours from the included charging case.
- Starting price: $579, described as limited-time pricing.
- Available through Ray-Ban.com, select Ray-Ban retail stores, Meta.com, and Meta Lab stores.

### 100+ glasses styles by end of 2026 (Meta Connect 2026)
What Meta says:
- Meta states it plans to offer more than 100 glasses options across Ray-Ban, Oakley, and Meta Glasses by the end of 2026, spanning audio-only, display, and camera-equipped models at a range of price points.

### How to use this at Touch Stone Publishers
1. Add hands-free Muse to the Content Creation Lab curriculum as the next-step example after yesterday's Meta Glasses launch. "The agent perceives what you're looking at and acts on it, without a phone" is a clean, concrete illustration for the module on where AI assistants are headed, and it is a wake-word interaction pattern attendees can compare against Grok Bot and ChatGPT's voice mode.
2. Treat Ray-Ban Meta Audio as a $579 field-recording option worth testing for the LinkedIn Live and Pryor course workflows. Audio-only, all-day battery with the case, and a built-in AI assistant is a lighter alternative to a phone or lapel mic for walk-and-talk content, though it should be benchmarked against existing recording gear before any purchase.
3. Note the 100+ glasses roadmap as a board-brief data point for the Technology and Consumer sector rotations: Meta is committing to price-tiered hardware variety rather than one flagship device, the same segmentation strategy phone makers use.
4. Hold off on any Muse-hands-free integration into TSP's own tools until Meta publishes developer-facing API details; today's announcements are consumer-facing, and nothing indicates a public API for the hands-free trigger yet.
