## 1. Anthropic

### Preparing for the 2026 US midterms (14 October 2026; not in earlier issues)
Source: https://www.anthropic.com/news/2026-us-midterms
What Anthropic says:
- Claude.ai is adding state-specific voting information drawn from Democracy Works and will show official election-night results. A TurboVote banner stays in place for registration and polling questions.
- Anthropic says its latest models scored 99 percent on its internal political evenhandedness evaluation, though outside reviewers still found Claude sometimes added unsolicited caveats.
- Red-teaming by the Institute for Strategic Dialogue and the McCain Institute found Claude reliably refused single harmful election-related requests but could miss misuse split into innocuous-looking steps. Anthropic says it responded with new classifiers for this kind of multi-step misuse, and that it banned accounts behind a foreign-run fake news site producing midterm coverage.

### An update on independent evaluators (updated 15 October 2026; not in earlier issues)
Source: https://www.anthropic.com/news/an-update-on-independent-evaluators
What Anthropic says:
- Anthropic is giving the nonprofit evaluator METR employee-like access to its models, with publication rights similar to a second-tier evaluator and only narrow, predefined categories where Anthropic can require redactions.
- SecureBio is reviewing the chemical and biological weapons chapter of Anthropic's August Risk Report, with possible expanded access to follow.
- A new Accenture partnership, led by Faculty, Accenture's AI business, will cover red-teaming, alignment assessments, and safeguard testing. Anthropic and Accenture each plan to invest at least $1 billion over five years. Anthropic says other organizations, including Hugging Face, are in early discussions to join.

### How to use this at Touch Stone Publishers
1. Log the midterms safeguards post as a board-brief and sector-brief data point on how frontier labs handle election-season misuse, continuing the running thread on AI governance and safety transparency from recent issues.
2. Note the independent-evaluator expansion, especially the Accenture deal's scale, as a sector-brief comparison point on how AI vendors are building outside verification into their safety programs rather than self-certifying alone.

## 2. OpenAI

### GPT-Live-1.1: new voices and better accents (15 October 2026; not in earlier issues)
Source: https://community.openai.com/t/gpt-live-1-1-is-rolling-out-with-new-voices-and-better-accents/1406704
What OpenAI says:
- GPT-Live-1.1 rolled out to all ChatGPT Voice users on 15 October, with API availability to follow. OpenAI describes the update as adding more natural accents across languages.
- New voice options were added for French, German, Hindi, Japanese, Korean, and Mexican Spanish, plus a new English voice called Bubbie. Available voices may vary by region and account.

### How to use this at Touch Stone Publishers
1. If any TSP workflow tests ChatGPT Voice for non-English audiences, such as a Content Creation Lab pilot aimed at Spanish-speaking small businesses, re-test voice quality now that OpenAI says accents have improved.
