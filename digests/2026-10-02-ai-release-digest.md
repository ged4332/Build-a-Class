## 2. OpenAI

### ChatGPT Pro 500 and GPT-6 Astra Ultrafast (29 September 2026, DevDay; not in the 30 September issue)
Source: https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9793128-about-chatgpt-pro-tiers
What OpenAI says:
- Pro 500 is a new $500 a month ChatGPT plan with the highest usage allowance among Pro plans, 25 times the ChatGPT Plus allowance, and the only Pro tier with access to Ultrafast.
- GPT-6 Astra Ultrafast is available today in the API and in ChatGPT Work and Codex on Pro 500 and Enterprise plans. Ultrafast mode for GPT-6 Astra is broadly available to all API users at low rate limits, offering up to 8 times faster token generation in Codex and up to 6 times faster in the API.
- Pro 500 is available through ChatGPT on the web at launch and is billed monthly, with no annual billing or multi-month prepay option.

### How to use this at Touch Stone Publishers
1. Treat Pro 500 as a narrow fit, not a default upgrade. The 25x usage allowance and Ultrafast access matter only if Glenn is running sustained, high-volume Codex or Work sessions; for the Build-a-Class site's current workload, the standard Pro plan likely still covers it.
2. If the Build-a-Class site ever needs a same-day turnaround on a large Codex task, Astra Ultrafast's up to 8x speed in Codex is the lever to reach for, worth testing once before deciding if Pro 500 pays for itself.
