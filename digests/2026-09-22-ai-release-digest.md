## 2. OpenAI

### Astra for Law (17 September 2026; not in earlier issues)
Sources: https://openai.com/index/astra-for-law/ and https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001528-astra-for-law
What OpenAI says:
- A foundation for law firms and legal technology companies to build AI products and workflows around their expertise, combining GPT-6 Astra with settings, tools, and context for professional legal work.
- A legal search index helps lawyers find the exact right authority, locate relevant passages, and understand how relevant and binding they are. It searches US case law, statutes, regulations, court rules, and administrative decisions across a corpus of more than 230 million URLs, with sources added daily.
- On OpenAI's evaluation, Astra for Law passed the overall correctness check on 54.0 percent of questions, against 38.7 percent for GPT-6 Astra using web search alone, a 40 percent relative improvement.
- 26 new ecosystem plugins connect ChatGPT to specialist legal tools firms already use, including Relativity and Clio.
- Availability: initially offered to selected US law firms through Trusted Access and Codex, with API access coming soon.

### Australian Youth Safety Blueprint (18 September 2026; not in earlier issues)
Source: https://openai.com/index/australian-youth-safety-blueprint/
What OpenAI says:
- A roadmap for protecting young people using AI, framed as a contribution to Australian policy. Sets out six pillars: AI literacy, age-appropriate safeguards, privacy-protective age assurance, connections to real-world crisis support, and accessible parental controls, among others.
- Follows the August rollout of ChatGPT for Teens in Australia, a default experience for users identified as 13 to 17 with safeguards built around their developmental needs.
- Policy document, not a product release. Included here because it signals compliance requirements for any business serving under-18 users through ChatGPT.

### How to use this at Touch Stone Publishers
1. Read Astra for Law as this week's sector intel signal for legal and professional services, not a tool to adopt. A 230-million-URL case law index with citation-level accuracy is a board-level shift in how law firms staff research, and it is a template for what a vertical-specific Astra product looks like.
2. Note the ecosystem-plugin pattern again. Relativity and Clio joining as plugins is the same packaging model as the Financial Services and education plugins from earlier issues. If Touch Stone ever pitches a TSP-branded plugin, this is the growing precedent list to cite.
3. If the Content Creation Lab ever serves attendees under 18, review the Australian Youth Safety Blueprint's six pillars against the lab's own intake and consent language, even though the lab is not in Australia. The pillars are a reasonable checklist for any AI-assisted program that could reach minors.

## 4. xAI (Grok)

### Grok 4.7 (21 September 2026)
Sources: https://x.ai/news/grok-4-7 and https://docs.x.ai/developers/grok-4-7
What xAI says:
- "The most capable model for coding and knowledge work" from xAI to date. Works longer on difficult tasks, checks its own work more carefully, and carries xAI's best-calibrated safeguards yet.
- Built on a new, larger base model than Grok 4.6, trained with a longer reinforcement learning run on a harder mix of tasks weighted toward problems that take many hours to complete.
- Pricing unchanged from Grok 4.6: $2 per million input tokens and $6 per million output tokens. A fast variant costs twice that.
- Available in Cursor, Grok Build, the xAI API, third-party coding harnesses, and model routers and cloud platforms.

### X Search API price change (effective 21 September 2026, 12:00 PM PT)
Source: https://docs.x.ai/developers/pricing
- X Search is now billed at $5 per 1,000 posts fetched and $10 per 1,000 user profiles fetched, replacing the previous flat $5 per 1,000 calls.

### How to use this at Touch Stone Publishers
1. Add Grok 4.7 to the three-way Red Team bake-off from earlier issues, alongside Fable 5.1 and GPT-6 Astra. Same price as Grok 4.6, so this is a pure quality upgrade to test on the board and sector brief drafting stage.
2. Re-check any workflow that uses X Search for sentiment signals in the MBB Sentiment silo of the board brief. The new per-post and per-profile pricing can change the cost of a wide social scan versus a narrow one; a targeted profile pull is now priced differently from a broad post search.
3. Use Grok 4.7's longer-horizon reasoning for the hardest single task in a Pryor course build, the instructional-design red team pass, and compare its findings against the existing pryor-lab-redteam skill output.
