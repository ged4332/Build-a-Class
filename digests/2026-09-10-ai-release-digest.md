## 2. OpenAI

### ChatGPT Images 2.5 (8 September 2026; not in the 9 September issue)
Sources: https://openai.com/index/introducing-chatgpt-images-2-5/ and https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models/gpt-image-2.5-flare and https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models/gpt-image-2.5-sunburst
What OpenAI says:
- Available to all ChatGPT, ChatGPT Work, and Codex users across desktop, mobile, and web.
- Image generation latency reduced by up to 50 percent compared with Images 2.0.
- More natural lighting and richer textures, better at preserving the subjects in reference photos, and follows editing instructions more reliably across multiple turns.
- Sketch: a new feature that lets you draw directly in ChatGPT as a reference for the final image.
- API: two new models. GPT-Image-2.5 Flare carries the same quality, editing, and speed improvements. GPT-Image-2.5 Sunburst offers an extra level of precision for detailed creative work with longer generation times.
- API pricing: image output is $30 per million tokens for both Flare and Sunburst. Text output is not billed because the models output images. Input token rates were not in the search results; check the pricing page.

### GPT-6 Astra: "The next generation in intelligence for work" (9 September 2026)
Sources: https://openai.com/index/gpt-6-astra/ and https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001354-gpt-56-and-gpt-6-pro-in-chatgpt
What OpenAI says:
- "The most capable model OpenAI has ever broadly deployed." Improvements in coding, research, computer use, and complex multi-step work.
- Astra can create documents, spreadsheets, and presentations that follow your templates and instructions, and adapt when you add requirements or change direction.
- Makes substantially fewer factual errors than GPT-5.6 Sol and is significantly less likely to reproduce user-reported hallucinations.
- Plan access: GPT-6 Pro, powered by GPT-6 Astra, is rolling out in ChatGPT for Pro $100, Pro $200, Business, and Enterprise. Plus plans include GPT-6 Astra in ChatGPT Work and Codex as it rolls out. OpenAI's 3 September post also said Astra would reach all Plus, Pro, Business, and Enterprise users plus the API, Azure, and AWS Bedrock over the coming days.
- Safety monitoring can pause or stop a conversation when an agent may have misread instructions, for the user to review.
- Note: threads on OpenAI's own developer forum are asking for clarification because Plus access is currently through ChatGPT Work and Codex rather than the main chat. That is forum discussion, not an OpenAI statement, and is noted here only so the plan-access line is read carefully.

### How to use this at Touch Stone Publishers
1. Move image generation to Images 2.5 now. The 9 September issue flagged that the DALL·E GPT retired on 30 August. Flare is the replacement for featured images on the board and sector briefs, and multi-turn editing means one composite can be iterated instead of regenerated.
2. Use reference-photo preservation for the lab creative. Testimonial and before-and-after composites for the Content Creation Lab depend on the subject staying recognizable. That is the specific improvement OpenAI claims.
3. Reserve Sunburst for print. The Lulu cover and any playbook interior visuals justify the longer generation time. Everything social goes through Flare.
4. Try Sketch for slide layouts. Draw the block layout for a Pryor slide or a LinkedIn banner and let the model finish it. Faster than describing the composition in words.
5. Test Astra's document claim on a real deliverable. It says it follows your templates. Feed it the TSP facilitator guide template and a course data file, and compare the output against the pryor-course-builder engine on the completeness gate.
6. Check which door Astra opens for your plan. On Pro, Business, or Enterprise it is GPT-6 Pro in the picker. On Plus it is inside ChatGPT Work and Codex. Confirm before assuming it is in the main chat.

## Cross-vendor takeaways

1. Image models are the quiet cost lever this month. Images 2.5 Flare at $30 per million output tokens, Muse Image inside Meta AI, and Grok Imagine's new aspect ratios give three options for the featured-image step in every brief. Pick per channel, not per vendor.
2. Frontier access is now plan-shaped. Astra on Plus arrives through Work and Codex, on Pro through GPT-6 Pro. Fable 5.1 sits on Pro and above. The subscription tier decides which model a workflow can use as much as the API does.
