## 1. Anthropic

### Singapore office and government MOU (14 October 2026)
Source: https://www.anthropic.com/news/singapore-office-opening
What Anthropic says:
- Anthropic opened a new Singapore office, its fifth in the Asia-Pacific region, and signed a memorandum of understanding with Singapore's Ministry of Digital Development and Information, signed at Singapore International Cyber Week.
- The MOU covers AI safety, security, and model evaluation, research on AI adoption and its impact, and responsible adoption across the public and private sectors. Anthropic plans technical exchanges with Singapore's AI safety, security, and assurance institutions on model evaluation, testing standards, and incident response.
- Anthropic names GovTech Singapore, Grab, and DBS as organizations already using Claude, and says the Cyber Security Agency of Singapore is exploring how Claude could strengthen the country's cyber defense. A similar MOU was already in place with Korea's Ministry of Science and ICT, announced with Anthropic's Seoul office.

## 3. Meta

### Introducing Muse Wonder and Muse Wonder Full: frontier models for agents (12 October 2026; not in earlier issues)
Source: https://about.fb.com/news/2026/10/introducing-muse-wonder-models/
What Meta says:
- Muse Wonder and Muse Wonder Full are Meta's most capable models to date, built for agentic work. Muse Wonder is available today through the Meta Model API and Muse Code; Muse Wonder Full is available today for Muse Code subscribers only, with no separate API price.
- Muse Wonder API pricing: a 1 million token context window, $4.00 input, $0.20 cached input, and $20.00 output, stated per million tokens.
- Muse Code subscription plans: Power Usage at $50 per month and Maximum Usage at $200 per month, with Maximum offering 80 times more usage than the Everyday Usage plan. Meta says Muse Wonder will soon power experiences in the consumer Muse app, rolling out first to subscribers.

1. Treat Muse Wonder's agentic focus and its Muse Code subscription tiers as a sector-brief comparison point against Claude and GPT-6 Astra on pricing and positioning for agent workloads, useful context for any board brief on the competitive AI landscape.
