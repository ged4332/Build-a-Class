## 2. OpenAI

### Live voice now supports plugins, plus a redesigned home screen (23 September 2026; not in earlier issues)
Sources: https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6825453-chatgpt-release-notes
What OpenAI says:
- Live now supports plugins on web, iOS, and Android. Users can use the plugins and connected apps available to their account during a voice conversation and follow written responses in the chat.
- Voice is also available in Work on web and mobile, where it can create documents, presentations, and spreadsheets, use connected apps, or work in a browser.
- A redesigned home screen, including a new iPad split view that keeps the task list beside the open task in landscape.
- A new way to search chats, projects, images, and documents on web, iOS, and Android, with filters to narrow results by content type.
- New Zendesk and OneNote plugins added to the plugin directory. The Zendesk plugin helps teams review support tickets and customer history; the OneNote plugin helps find and summarize notes.

### How to use this at Touch Stone Publishers
1. Test Live's new plugin support for the outreach-review loop. Talking through a batch of drafts by voice while ChatGPT pulls from a connected CRM or notes app, instead of typing, is a faster way to work through the morning triage list.
2. Try the OneNote plugin against the Pryor course facilitator guides if any of that source material lives in OneNote. It is a zero-build way to search and summarize existing notes rather than re-typing them into a prompt.
3. Use the new cross-content search on web and iOS to pull up prior board briefs, sector briefs, and Pryor builds by keyword instead of scrolling chat history, now that search spans chats, projects, images, and documents in one place.

## 3. Meta

Meta Connect 2026 closed on 24 September. Several announcements from the event, and one from mid-September, had not surfaced in earlier issues.

### Meta One subscription (mid-September 2026, Meta's own page places it in the September 15 to 16 window; not in earlier issues)
Sources: https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/introducing-meta-one-subscription-service-more-features-ai/ and https://www.meta.com/meta-one-plans/
What Meta says:
- A new subscription service across Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp, and Meta AI offering higher AI usage, enhanced expression features, and professional tools for creators and businesses. The core experience across the apps and Meta AI stays free.
- Two bundle tiers: Core at $7.99 a month and Premium at $19.99 a month. Core adds more Restyle uses on Instagram, more voice effects and creative tools, more Meta AI image and video generation, and expanded individual app features (Instagram Plus, WhatsApp Plus, Facebook Plus). Premium includes everything in Core plus the largest monthly AI usage allowance, including advanced image and video models powered by Muse.
- Launched with more than 50 features across the four apps, with AI glasses and Edits named as coming later. Meta states 15 million subscriptions and trials to date.
- This is the pricing detail earlier issues flagged as not yet stated by Meta.

### Ray-Ban Meta (Gen 3) (24 September 2026; not in earlier issues)
Source: https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/the-biggest-news-from-connect-2026/
What Meta says:
- The next generation of Meta's best-selling AI glasses, with a slimmer, more comfortable design than Gen 2, up to 9 hours of battery life on a charge, plus 50 more hours from the included charging case.
- A new customizable action button gives one-tap access to Meta AI, texting a favorite contact, translation, or another assigned feature.
- Available today, starting at $449.

### Hearing enhancement for AI glasses (24 September 2026; not in earlier issues)
Source: https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/the-biggest-news-from-connect-2026/
What Meta says:
- An FDA-cleared software feature that helps adults 18 and older with perceived mild to moderate hearing loss hear better, added to supported AI glasses. Setup takes minutes at home, with no clinic visit or prescription needed.
- Available for $149.99, HSA and FSA eligible, or included with a Meta One subscription. Launching in the US later this year.

### New features for Meta Ray-Ban Display (24 September 2026; not in earlier issues)
Source: https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/new-features-for-meta-ray-ban-display-navigation-hologram/
What Meta says:
- Hands-free cycling and public transit directions, asked for by voice, with turn-by-turn cycling directions or real-time transit departures shown on the display.
- Walking directions that reference what is in front of the wearer, for example "turn left at the yellow house," rather than street names.
- A photorealistic, voice-driven hologram of the wearer's face for video calls, generated from a short self-capture in the Meta AI app and driven entirely by voice and speech patterns. Beginning to roll out in Early Access on WhatsApp to Meta Ray-Ban Display users in the US later this fall.

### How to use this at Touch Stone Publishers
1. Decide whether a Meta One Premium seat is worth trialing for the marketing engine. At $19.99 a month for the largest Muse-powered image and video allowance, it is a direct input cost comparison against the Higgsfield workflow already used for lab and flagship visuals.
2. Note Meta One resolves last month's open pricing question. Earlier issues flagged that Muse subscription pricing was not yet public; $7.99 Core and $19.99 Premium is the number to plug into any Meta-AI cost comparison going forward.
3. Treat Ray-Ban Meta Gen 3's action button as a Content Creation Lab talking point on hardware-level AI shortcuts, a one-tap trigger rather than a wake word, worth contrasting with hands-free Muse from yesterday's issue.
4. File the hearing enhancement feature and the Meta Ray-Ban Display hologram as sector-brief material for the Technology and Consumer rotations. An FDA-cleared feature sold through a consumer subscription, and a voice-driven hologram entering WhatsApp video calls, are both board-level signals about where AI hardware and health-adjacent regulation are heading.
5. Hold off on any Ray-Ban Display hologram use for TSP client calls until it leaves Early Access; it is US-only and WhatsApp-only for now.
