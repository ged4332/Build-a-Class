## 1. Anthropic

### Expanding the Cyber Verification Program (6 October 2026)
Source: https://www.anthropic.com/news/cyber-verification-program
What Anthropic says:
- The Cyber Verification Program now makes advanced cyber capabilities and reduced blocking classifiers available to qualifying security professionals across three access tiers.
- Defense Access covers defensive work, security operations center and incident response tasks, malware reverse-engineering, and vulnerability analysis and validation, open to security teams at companies, nonprofits, universities, and government bodies, critical infrastructure operators, smaller security firms, open-source maintainers, and individual researchers with a reported-vulnerability track record. Anthropic aims to respond within a few days.
- Red Team Access adds authorized penetration testing and red-teaming, for in-house red teams, government red teams, and security and penetration testing firms, with review taking a few weeks.
- Specialized Access, with the fewest cyber blocks, is reserved for a limited set of verified organizations authorized to test safety systems that could affect lives or markets, reviewed in depth with the US government.
- Every tier includes access to Claude Opus 5.5, Claude Sonnet 5.5, and Claude Mythos 5.1, plus future models. Anthropic states its Project Glasswing partners uncovered at least 129,000 verified software vulnerabilities between April and July 2026.

### How to use this at Touch Stone Publishers
1. Treat the 129,000-vulnerability figure as a citable board-brief and sector-brief data point for the Technology rotation on what AI-assisted vulnerability research is already finding at scale.
2. Note the tiered-verification structure as a second example, after the Life Sciences Verification Program from 27 September, of Anthropic gating its most permissive safeguards behind credentialed access rather than opening them broadly. A recurring pattern worth naming the next time a brief covers AI governance models.

## 3. Meta

### Muse Realtime Avatar (Meta Connect 2026 week; not in earlier issues)
Source: https://www.meta.com/blog/meta-connect-2026-everything-we-announced/
What Meta says:
- Meta Superintelligence Labs introduced Muse Realtime Avatar, embodiment technology that turns Muse Realtime Voice into an expressive, interactive avatar carrying Muse Spark's voice and intelligence.
- The model generates video in real time, keeping facial expressions and lip movements synchronized so Muse appears visually consistent during a conversation.
- Positioned alongside Meta's broader move to bring Muse to AI glasses, so the agent is seen, heard, and spoken to across more places in daily life, not just on phone or desktop screens.

### How to use this at Touch Stone Publishers
1. File this as a Content Creation Lab talking point on where consumer AI agents are headed, a real-time, lip-synced visual presence rather than text or voice alone, worth one slide alongside the hands-free Muse-on-glasses material from the 24 September issue.
